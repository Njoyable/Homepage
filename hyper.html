Suppose a population contains N items. Of those, M have a specific characteristic.

You draw n items without replacement. This calculator determines the probability that exactly k of the drawn items have that characteristic.

Formula
\( P(X = k) = \frac{ \binom{M}{k} \cdot \binom{N-M}{n-k} }{ \binom{N}{n} } \)
